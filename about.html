Canon

A Bible reading program where you can't get behind

About

- Shared by Jami on The Literary Life Discord - https://ransomfellowship.org/article/bible-reading-program-for-slackers-shirkers/ - https://archive.org/details/ifgodalreadyknow00kell/page/194/mode/2up A program where you can't get behind Douglas Kelly says it has existed since the Puritans. The sections are not equal lengths. When you finish a section, that day becomes a day to read one of the longest remaining sections. If you complete each day's reading, you will finish the Bible in xxx days. How do I catch up? There is no "behind" so there is no "catching up".
